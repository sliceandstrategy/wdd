Overview

Purpose

The purpose of the Beehive Rehabilitation & Counseling website is to provide accessible, up-to-date information about outpatient rehabilitation and counseling services to individuals and families seeking support for substance use, mental health challenges, and co-occurring disorders. The site serves as a trusted resource where visitors can learn about available treatment programs, meet the counseling team, understand the recovery process, and easily connect to professional help. It also aims to offer educational materials, streamline referrals, and ensure that clients can quickly find contact details, service options, and support tailored to their unique recovery journey.

Audience

The audience of the Beehive Rehabilitation & Counseling website includes individuals actively seeking information about substance use treatment, mental health support, and recovery services. Many visitors may be struggling with addiction or co-occurring disorders and are looking for accessible, local help. Others may be family members or friends trying to find resources and support for their loved ones. The site also serves healthcare providers, social workers, and legal professionals who need a reliable place to refer clients. Overall, the website is designed for people who need clear, trustworthy information about available services, how to get started, and how Beehive can support their recovery journey

Dynamic elements

The Beehive Rehabilitation & Counseling website will use JavaScript to create dynamic, interactive elements that improve user experience. A key feature will be an online contact form that allows users to easily submit inquiries or request appointments directly through the website. The site will also include smooth scrolling animations that guide visitors through sections of the page in an engaging way as they navigate. Additionally, an FAQ accordion menu will be provided to help users quickly find answers to common questions. This menu will expand and collapse each question with a simple click, making the information easy to browse without overwhelming the page.
